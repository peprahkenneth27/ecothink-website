About ECOTHINK

Research that ends up on the land

Our mission

Humans live in harmony with their ecosystems.

The Ecosystem Thinking Research and Development Group (ECOTHINK) is a not-for-profit think tank and action research collective based in Wa, Upper West Region, Ghana. It was incorporated in 2024 as a company limited by guarantee and is licensed as a National Non-Profit Organisation.

ECOTHINK brings together senior academics and practitioners from UBIDS, Dr. Hilla Limann Technical University and the University for Development Studies, whose combined experience spans ecosystem management, engineering, gender and social inclusion, geography and development planning.

We drive sustainable development and environmental stewardship across Sub-Saharan Africa. Our strength is closing the gap between what academic research knows and what communities, districts and partners can put into practice.

Our work contributes to ecosystem-based management, climate change resilience and better human well-being in Ghana and beyond. We treat environmental health and social well-being, including child protection and sanitation, as parts of one system.

Experience our team brings

Pioneering regional planning on the Black Volta

We won and executed an IUCN project to develop a participatory five-year development plan for Nandom District, along the ecologically sensitive Black Volta River. Ecosystem management was integrated directly into local governance.

Projects

PGIS mapping for GIZ completed across Upper West and Savannah

District Assemblies now have participatory maps of their development infrastructure.

Read more

Working with communities and district officers, our team mapped schools, health facilities, water points, roads and other infrastructure. The data supports strategic planning and helps target investment where it is needed most.
